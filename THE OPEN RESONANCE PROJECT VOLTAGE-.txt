ABSTRACT
This paper outlines a low-cost, open-source, non-invasive therapeutic system designed to stimulate localized cellular regeneration, microvascular angiogenesis, and tissue repair. The method completely bypasses invasive surgeries and expensive synthetic pharmaceuticals. It relies on a two-part system: an ingestible/topical bio-absorbable piezoelectric micro-scaffold that self-assembles at the site of tissue damage via electrostatic attraction, and an external wearable resonance device that activates the scaffold using low-frequency electromagnetic or acoustic fields (specifically utilizing the 7.83 Hz Schumann Resonance baseline).
1. THE INGESTIBLE TARGET: ELECTROSTATIC HOMING MECHANISM
Healthy tissue maintains a balanced bio-electric membrane potential. When tissue experiences trauma, chronic inflammation, vascular blockage, or ischemic events, the damaged cells enter a state of metabolic distress. This distress shifts the outer membrane charge of the injured zone, generating a highly concentrated negative electrical charge (ζ-potential).
This protocol utilizes an organic, kitchen-manufacturable cationic (positively charged) biopolymer matrix to exploit this biological vulnerability:
• The Structural Core: Liquid silk fibroin extracted from raw silk fibers forms a highly flexible, biocompatible polymer chain.
• The Charge Carrier: Food-grade chitosan (derived from mushroom or chitin cell walls) is blended into the matrix. The abundant free amino groups provide a permanent, resilient positive electrical charge.
• Self-Assembly: When ingested or administered, these micro-spheres flow passively through the body. Bypassing healthy tissue, they bond directly to the negatively charged, inflamed injury sites via electrostatic attraction, forming a localized, passive mechanical scaffold directly over the wound.

The self-assembled micro-spheres remain completely passive and inert inside the body until they interact with the external wearable device.

4. COMPREHENSIVE DIY MANUFACTURING PROTOCOL (OPEN-SOURCE SPECS)
To ensure universal affordability, the biological target matrix is manufactured using open-access kitchen chemistry:
• Extraction: Raw silk fibers are boiled in a 0.02M solution of sodium carbonate (pure wood ash or washing soda) in distilled water for 30 minutes to strip away sericin, isolating pure liquid silk fibroin.
• Gelation: The isolated liquid silk is mixed with a 2% food-grade sodium alginate solution and a 1% dissolved chitosan solution.
• Cross-Linking: The blended fluid is passed through a standard fine droplet misting nozzle into a bath of 1% calcium chloride (common pickling crisp). The droplets instantly cross-link, forming uniform, positively charged, piezoelectric micro-spheres ready for consumption.
